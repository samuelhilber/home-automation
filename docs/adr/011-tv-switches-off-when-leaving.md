# ADR-011: The TV switches off after 15 minutes at the latest, or earlier when Samuel leaves home

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-03

## Context

Samuel's answer on 3 Nov 2026.

## Decision

The TV switches off after 15 minutes at the latest, or earlier when Samuel leaves home, detected by the Home Assistant iPhone app.

## Consequences

Needs remote access, because the phone reports leaving home over the internet (ADR-008).
