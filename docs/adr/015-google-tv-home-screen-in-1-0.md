# ADR-015: The TV keeps the normal Google TV home screen in 1.0

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-03

## Context

Samuel's choice on 3 Nov 2026. The morning screen runs in Fully Kiosk and needs no launcher. Projectivy replaces the home screen, which belongs to the 2.0 goal of an own TV interface. Projectivy is free, premium optional.

## Decision

The TV keeps the normal Google TV home screen in 1.0. Projectivy Launcher is tested in 2.0.

## Consequences

None recorded yet.
